## Selected Approach: Studio Field Notes

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design blended with the material language of an independent art studio: field journals, exhibition labels, studio walls, and annotated sketchbooks.

### Core Principles
1. Trust before novelty: visitors should understand the offer and next step quickly.
2. Creative but not childish: use tactile colour and art references without cartoon clichés.
3. Editorial asymmetry: use a split narrative-and-action hero instead of a generic centered SaaS layout.
4. Evidence of care: use labels, numbering, process notes, and privacy language to make the experience considered.

### Color Philosophy
Ink green `#153D35` anchors the brand with calm, care, and professional judgment. Cream paper `#F3F0E8` creates a quiet reading surface. Clay orange `#E76F3C` is reserved for actions, progress, and encouraging moments. Supporting sky blue and pencil yellow appear only as restrained annotation accents, never as gradients.

### Layout Paradigm
A briefing-flow structure: compact masthead, split hero with editorial statement and offset studio card, horizontal process notes, a craft-category strip, then an application desk section. Mobile collapses into a single-column sequence with clear anchors.

### Signature Elements
- Fine field-line rules and numbered index markers.
- Dossier labels such as `STUDIO NOTE / 01` and `FOR MAKERS + LEARNERS`.
- An open-C mark made from two offset lines, echoing a brush stroke and a parenthesis.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction lowers uncertainty. CTAs scroll to the application desk, navigation links anchor to sections, and hover states feel like a pencil mark or paper lift. No essential information depends on hover.

### Animation
Use subtle entrance opacity and 12–18px translate transitions with 40–60ms staggered delays. Buttons respond with a 150ms press scale and a small paper-lift shadow. Avoid looping motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing entrance transforms.

### Typography System
Headlines use DM Serif Display with generous line height and occasional italic emphasis. Interface text uses Manrope at 400–800 weights. Chinese fallback uses Noto Sans TC, PingFang TC, Microsoft JhengHei, sans-serif. Eyebrows use compact uppercase Manrope with letter spacing.

### Brand Essence
A thoughtful art-coach matching desk for people who want the right creative guide for their next step — more personal than a directory, clearer than a generic enquiry form. Personality: attentive, curious, grounded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are observant and encouraging. CTAs are specific and low-pressure. Microcopy explains what happens next without promising placement, income, or outcomes.

Example lines:
- “The right creative guide makes room for your way of seeing.”
- “Tell us what you love making. We’ll help shape the next good fit.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use an open capital C built from two offset studio lines: one ink-green line and one clay-orange line. Pair the symbol with a compact sans label and a serif wordmark rather than a default text logo.

### Signature Brand Color
Clay Orange `#E76F3C`.

### Similarity Boundary
This project intentionally follows the original site's broad information architecture and warm editorial spirit, while using independently written copy, newly generated visuals, and a distinct implementation.
